**Key Issue:**

- BUT THEN uses the ORIGINAL hostname string (which could have been spoofed via DNS rebinding) to construct the URL

- Since Java's `URL` constructor accepts hostnames directly rather than pre-resolved addresses, the second operation bypasses the initial DNS validation entirely
